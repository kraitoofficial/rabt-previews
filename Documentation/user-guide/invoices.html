Invoicing & Financial Operations

Create branded invoices with automated inventory deduction, PDF generation, email dispatch, and QR verification.

Invoice Status Workflow

Status Action Trigger Inventory Impact
Draft Initial creation; items and prices being edited None (Reserved/Quotation state)
Due Finalized invoice issued to customer with a due date Stock deducted via atomic Supabase RPC
Sent Dispatched via SMTP email with PDF attachment Stock remains deducted
Paid Payment received and recorded with date & method Settled
Overdue Past due date without complete payment Enters automated reminder sequence
Void Cancelled or refunded invoice Stock automatically restocked to inventory

Live Inventory Integration

When adding line items to an invoice, the system offers an intelligent inventory picker:

  • Type any product name or SKU to view real-time available stock.
  • Selecting a product automatically inserts its designated selling price and unit (e.g., pcs, hrs, kg).
  • Line item taxes and percentage/fixed discounts are calculated in real time.
  • When finalized, Postgres RPC reduce_inventory_stock atomically reduces warehouse quantities to prevent overselling.

Pixel-Perfect PDF Generation

Rabt employs a dual-engine architecture for PDF creation:

  • Headless Chromium (Puppeteer): Renders the invoice HTML template at 300 DPI print quality, including organization logos, custom fonts, and styling.
  • Fail-Safe Engine (pdf-lib): Seamless fallback in constrained environments to guarantee that invoice PDFs are always generated without failure.

Public Invoice QR Verification

Every generated invoice includes an encrypted QR code placed at the bottom:

Scanning the QR code redirects to the public route /verify/invoice. Clients and auditors can independently verify the invoice number, issue date, issuer organization, and total balance without needing to log in.

SMTP Email Dispatch

Send invoices directly to client emails from within the Rabt modal:

  • Automatically attaches the rendered PDF document.
  • Pre-fills client email from contact records.
  • Uses organization SMTP configuration defined in Admin Settings.
  • Updates status to Sent upon email provider confirmation.
